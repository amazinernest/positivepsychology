Programmes and activities

What the Association does, as set out in its constitution, and the programmes it has run since 2020.

01

Biennial conference

The Association’s flagship gathering brings together scholars, practitioners, policy makers and students. The 1st Biennial Conference was held at the University of Ibadan on 19–21 June 2023 under the theme Positive Psychology in Challenging Times. The 2nd followed on 16–18 June 2025 at Lagos State University of Education, Epe Campus, under the theme Thriving in a Changing World: Harnessing Resilience and Connection.

Each conference has combined a pre-conference skills session, syndicate sessions on its sub-themes, lead papers and a keynote address, with the annual general meeting held during the programme.

02

Annual convention and general meeting

The constitution provides for an annual convention at a time and place determined by the Executive and approved by the Board of Trustees. Joint meetings with related societies are subject to the Board’s approval.

The Board of Trustees meets in person at the annual conference, and the Executive reports to the Board at the annual general meeting. The 2023 conference programme included the AGM on 21 June.

03

Skills acquisition and professional development

The Association exists in part to retrain and instil professionalism, to rebuild confidence and to activate mentorship among behaviour-change experts. Its objectives commit it to providing practical, evidence-based solutions and to assisting experts in skill acquisition.

At the 2023 conference, a pre-conference skills-acquisition session, free of charge, addressed early warning signs and strategic management of depression, led by Dr Omotayo Ajirotutu of the Neuro-Psychiatric Hospital, Yaba, Lagos.

04

Counselling and mental-health services

Among its objectives, the Association commits to offering e-counselling or face-to-face counselling by experts on mental-health-related issues.

Details of how to access these services have not yet been published. Please contact the Secretariat for enquiries.

05

Knowledge sharing and networking

PoPAN disseminates information on the concepts, principles and strategies of positive psychology, and brings together people with a common interest to discuss social issues that bear on it.

It also provides a platform for academic ideals, job vacancies, fellowship opportunities, scholarships, institutional admissions, professional advancement, publications, conferences, networking and mentoring.

06

Divisions and branches

Divisions may be formed to represent major scientific and professional interests. A division is established when the Board of Trustees recommends it, or when 50 or more members petition for one and the Board approves. Each division has its own Chairman and Secretary and reports annually to the National Executive.

The Association may also be certified in regions of the country as branches, each with a secretariat at a fixed location approved by the Board of Trustees.

A list of current divisions and branches will be published here when available.

Contribute to the programme

Members who wish to propose a seminar, workshop or division, or to partner with the Association, are invited to write to the Secretariat.
