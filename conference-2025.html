PoPAN 2025: 2nd Biennial Conference

Thriving in a Changing World: Harnessing Resilience and Connection.

Dates
Monday 16 – Wednesday 18 June 2025
Venue
Lecture Theatre III, Lagos State University of Education, Epe Campus, Odo Noforija
Organiser
Positive Psychology Association of Nigeria
About the conference

Resilience and connection in a changing world

The conference was set to delve into key topics such as psychological resilience, leadership in times of transformation, community building, and the role of technology in maintaining human connection.

Through keynotes, interactive workshops and networking opportunities, thought leaders, industry experts, innovators and behaviour change agents were invited to bring to bear practical tools for enhancing resilience, embracing change and cultivating deeper connections.

It was the second in the Association’s biennial series, following the 1st Biennial Conference at the University of Ibadan in June 2023. See the 2023 conference.

Sub-themes

Eleven areas of enquiry

  • Adolescence and youth resilience and connection
  • Security, resilience and connection
  • Workplace resilience and connection
  • School resilience and connection
  • Family resilience, bonding and connection
  • Mental health, resilience and connection
  • Migration: resilience in motion, change and connection
  • Gender: change, resilience and connection
  • Religious resilience and connection
  • Technological dynamism, resilience and connection
  • Special needs, inclusivity, resilience and connection
