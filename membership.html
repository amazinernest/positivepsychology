Membership

PoPAN welcomes scholars, researchers and practitioners of positive psychology, and organisations whose values are consistent with its own.

Membership categories

Four ways to belong

Article 6 of the constitution provides for individual and organisational members, and for Fellows, who are distinguished members honoured by the Association.

Individual

Individual member

Scholars, researchers and practitioners dedicated to the scientific study and evidence-based, ethical application of positive psychology.

Requires a Master’s degree or higher in any field of psychology (for example clinical, counselling or educational) or an allied profession.

Organisational

Institutional member

Regional, national or international organisations, primarily non-profit, whose values and purposes are consistent with those of the Association.

The Board of Trustees sets the policies governing participation by member organisations.

Organisational

Corporate member

Regional, national or international for-profit organisations whose values and purposes are consistent with those of the Association.

The same Board policies apply as for institutional members.

By nomination

Fellow

Distinguished individuals who have made substantial contributions to positive psychology, nominated by an Awards Committee appointed by the Board of Trustees.

A Fellow is granted dues-free membership in the Association.

Affiliate organisations. Separately from membership, the Board of Trustees may accept as an Affiliate Organisation any regional, national or international organisation with consistent values and purposes. Affiliates may take part in PoPAN activities by agreement with the Board (Art. 8).

Eligibility

Who may join

  • Individuals must hold at least a Master’s degree in any field of psychology, or an allied profession, and be dedicated to the scientific study and ethical application of positive psychology.
  • Organisations must be regional, national or international bodies whose values and purposes are consistent with the Association’s. Non-profit organisations join as institutional members and for-profit organisations as corporate members.
  • Executive and Board members must be members in good standing of the Association.
Membership benefits

What membership offers

  • Take part in the Association’s conferences, annual convention and meetings.
  • Vote in elections for the Executive Committee and stand for office, as a member in good standing.
  • Join a Division that represents your scientific or professional interest.
  • Use a platform for academic ideals, job vacancies, fellowship opportunities, scholarships, institutional admissions, professional advancement, publications, conferences, networking and mentoring.
  • Be considered for Fellowship, which carries dues-free membership.
  • Benefit from fee discounts, where the Board of Trustees provides them for particular categories of members.
Dues and standing

Dues, suspension and appeal

All fees, including annual dues and any charges for participating in Association activities, are approved by the Board of Trustees. A fee schedule has not yet been published on this website; the Secretariat can confirm current dues.

Membership may be suspended or terminated by resignation, by non-payment of dues for two consecutive years, or by decision of the Board of Trustees where it has evidence of professional misconduct. A person whose membership is suspended or terminated is notified and may appeal in writing to the Board (Art. 7).

How to apply

Express your interest

A formal application form and procedure have not yet been published. Send an expression of interest to the Secretariat, which handles membership enquiries.

Individual applicants need at least a Master’s degree.

Submitting opens Gmail in a new tab with your message addressed to positivepopan@gmail.com, ready to send. Nothing is stored on this website.

Process

From interest to membership

  1. Confirm that you are eligible

    Check the eligibility requirements above for the category that fits you.

  2. Send an expression of interest

    Use the form on this page or write to positivepopan@gmail.com, stating your qualification, field and institution.

  3. Confirm documents and dues with the Secretariat

    Fees are set by the Board of Trustees. Ask the Secretariat for the current schedule and any supporting documents required.
