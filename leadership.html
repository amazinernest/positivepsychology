Leadership

The Executive Committee runs the Association day to day. The Board of Trustees is its stabilising force.

Executive Committee

The Executive Council

The Executive is responsible for the day-to-day running of the Association’s affairs and its finances, and reports to the Board of Trustees at the annual general meeting.

Executive members serve a two-year term and may be re-elected once, for a maximum of four years. The immediate past President is an ex-officio member.

Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees

The Board is the stabilising force in PoPAN. The constitution directs that trustees be drawn from leading scholars and respected practitioners of positive psychology.

The constitution provides for seven to nine trustees, in addition to ex-officio members. Each serves a single five-year term and may be re-elected for another. The President and the Secretary of the Association sit on the Board as ex-officio voting members.

Organisational structure

How the Association is governed

Authority flows from the general body of registered members, through its elected Executive, with the Board of Trustees providing oversight.

General body of registered members

Elects the President, Vice-President, Treasurer, Secretary, Assistant Secretary, Assistant Financial Secretary, Internal Auditor and Public Relations Officer (Art. 11.1).

Board of Trustees

Seven to nine members plus ex-officio members. Reviews the Executive’s annual reports, approves fees and policies for organisational members, and steps in to resolve any crisis in the Association.

Executive Committee

Manages the Association’s affairs and finances. Reports to the Board of Trustees at the annual general meeting.

Divisions and branches

Divisions represent major scientific and professional interests. Each is led by a Chairman and a Secretary, and each Division Chairman is an ex-officio member of the Board of Trustees.

Committees and elections

Committees provided for in the constitution

Nominating Committee
Appointed by the Board of Trustees from the Board and/or the Executive. Prepares the list of nominees for the Board and the Executive Committee (Art. 11.3).
Electoral Committee
Conducts elections by secret electronic ballot. An election is complete only when every member has had a reasonable opportunity to vote and a quorum of 50% plus one has voted (Art. 11.4).
Awards Committee
Appointed by the Board of Trustees. May nominate as Fellows distinguished individuals who have made substantial contributions to positive psychology (Art. 6.3).
Local Organising Committees
Constituted for individual events. For the 2023 conference it was chaired by Taiwo M. Williams, PhD, with Mr. Mohammed Ahmed Ademola as Secretary.

Divisions and branches are provided for in Articles 12 and 15 of the constitution. A list of current divisions will be published here when available.
