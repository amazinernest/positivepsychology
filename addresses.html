28 June 2020

Welcome address and maiden speech

Delivered at the maiden meeting of the Positive Psychology Association of Nigeria. Typographical errors in the original have been corrected.

My senior colleagues and colleagues,

I welcome you all to this maiden meeting of the Positive Psychology Association of Nigeria (PoPAN).

I am glad that it is a dream come true, and I am most delighted that 41 of us are part of this historic occasion.

Martin E.P. Seligman introduced and popularised positive psychology. Positive psychology is the scientific study of the strengths that enable individuals and communities to thrive. The field is founded on the belief that people want to lead meaningful and fulfilling lives, to cultivate what is best within themselves, and to enhance their experiences of love, work and play. The science of positive psychology operates on three different levels: the subjective level, the individual level and the group level.

As we all know, psychology is very broad and wide. This is largely because the evolving human being is dynamic and complex. Traditionally, several branches of psychology address the metapathologies of humankind, but positive psychology focuses more on how to enhance or foster metaneeds.

It is very sad to note that a large crop of our graduates in Nigeria with backgrounds in guidance and counselling, educational psychology, psychology and allied fields are not well equipped with the relevant skills to function in public and private practice. This has damaged their self-esteem and career self-efficacy. The good news is that PoPAN recognises that, as good as a university degree is in Nigeria today, it has its limits. PoPAN is here to retrain and instil professionalism, to rebuild confidence and to activate mentorship among behaviour-change experts for optimal performance.

We all belong to other professional bodies, such as the Nigerian Psychological Society, the Counselling Association of Nigeria, the Nigerian Society for Educational Psychologists and the Nigerian Association of Clinical Psychologists, among others. PoPAN is different and unique. Positive psychology has tremendous advantage for humanity. In the near future, once official registration of the Association is completed, Associate, Affiliate and Fellow membership courses, examinations and licensure will be introduced for PoPAN membership. We will hold annual or bi-annual conferences to advance the principles of positive psychology. Members will also be able to network and participate in various programmes and training of the International Positive Psychology Association (IPPA) at heavily discounted rates. With the support of IPPA, some countries have started running B.Sc., M.Sc. and PhD programmes in positive psychology.

Let me end this speech with four of Seligman’s quotations.

“Authentic happiness derives from raising the bar for yourself, not rating yourself against others.”

“Curing the negatives does not produce the positives.”

“Pessimistic labels lead to passivity, whereas optimistic ones lead to attempts to change.”

“The genius of evolution lies in the dynamic tension between optimism and pessimism continually correcting each other.”

Let us join hands to build this Association. Let us own it. It belongs to all of us. I am just a midwife.

D.A. Oluwole, PhDConvener, Positive Psychology Association of Nigeria
28 June 2020
19 June 2023

Welcome address, 1st Biennial Conference

Delivered at the opening of the 1st Biennial Conference, University of Ibadan. Typographical errors in the original have been corrected.

The Vice-Chancellor,
The Dean, Faculty of Education,
Your Excellencies,
Distinguished scholars present,
Gentlemen of the Press,
Ladies and Gentlemen.

I welcome you all to the 1st Biennial Conference of the Positive Psychology Association of Nigeria (PoPAN).

The Positive Psychology Association of Nigeria is a non-profit organisation established in 2020, exactly three years ago, with the main vision of promoting and integrating the science and application of positive psychology towards the advancement of people’s wellbeing locally and internationally. Positive psychologists pay more attention to building on positive behaviours and traits already present in individuals. Positive psychology interventions aim to boost positive emotions and help clients find meaning in life.

I am glad that another dream of this young association has become a reality. We intended to hold this conference in 2022, but the prolonged ASUU strike could not make it happen.

As we all know, psychology is very broad and wide. This is largely because the evolving human being is dynamic and complex. Traditionally, several branches of psychology address metapathologies in humankind, but positive psychology focuses more on entrenching proactive, intentional, meaningful living so as to generate happiness and life satisfaction.

As an association, the Positive Psychology Association of Nigeria is an affiliate of the International Positive Psychology Association (IPPA), which has members in over 70 countries and is committed to advancing the scientific study and ethical applications of positive psychology. Our association is a potpourri of experts from various subsets of psychology.

Today, a major challenge facing us in Nigeria is insecurity: economic, social, physical and emotional. For this reason, the theme of this year’s conference is “Positive Psychology in Challenging Times: Promoting national security through mental health”.

Nigerians have passed through a lot in the last ten years, and many people are suffering from mental breakdown. There is hardly a day when you will not meet someone who feels overwhelmed and unable to concentrate or make decisions; who is moody, feeling low or depressed; who feels burnt out; who has emotional outbursts of uncontrollable anger, fear, helplessness or crying; or who feels depersonalised, not like themselves or detached from situations.

We have invited experts in behaviour management to give us lectures on various ways to apply positive psychology in mitigating insecurity as well as sustaining our mental health. While the conference will last for three days, over 60 scholars have signified their intention to present papers on the various sub-themes. A high point of this year’s conference is the recognition and awards to well-deserving members of our society who have contributed immensely to capacity building and sustainable development in various human endeavours.

A popular saying is still valid: a tree cannot make a forest. As the founder and pioneer President of the Association, I feel honoured and humbled by the presence of dignitaries and scholars at this conference this morning. I deeply appreciate the support I enjoy from our Board of Trustees, chaired by Oyesoji Aremu. I appreciate the Executive members of the Association, the Local Organising Committee chaired by Dr Tolani Williams, and all members of the Association.

I wish us happy deliberations as we strive for social re-engineering in Nigeria.

Thank you, and God bless you immensely.

D.A. Oluwole, PhDConvener, Positive Psychology Association of Nigeria
19 June 2023
