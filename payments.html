Payments and dues

Pay membership registration, annual renewal, journal publication charges or a donation by bank transfer, then notify the Secretariat so your payment can be recorded.

What you can pay for

Fellows are granted dues-free membership under the constitution. Fees for institutional and corporate members are confirmed by the Secretariat.

Process

From payment to record

  1. Pay by bank transfer or deposit

    Use the account above and keep your receipt or transfer confirmation.

  2. Notify the Secretariat

    Complete the payment form on this page. It opens a ready-to-send Gmail message with your details.

  3. Attach your payment evidence

    Attach a photo or screenshot of your receipt to that email before sending it.

  4. The Secretariat records your payment

    Once confirmed, your dues are recorded on the Member register and, for new members, a membership certificate is issued.

Payment notification

I have made a payment

Submitting opens Gmail in a new tab with this notification addressed to positivepopan@gmail.com. Attach your receipt before sending. This form does not process payments and nothing is stored on this website.
